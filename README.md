### 1:1 — In the hand

**[Portable Subway Telltale](https://github.com/Daidai1031/Portable-Subway-Telltale)** · `Python` `ESP32`
A keychain-sized ambient display for Roosevelt Island station. No app, no notification — you glance at it on the way out the door and you know whether to run.

<br>

### 1:5 — On the table

**[PROMPT!](https://github.com/Daidai1031/PROMPT-)** · `Jetson` `Whisper` `Llama 3` `Kokoro TTS`
A voice-first AI literacy card game for kids 10+. They read a card, say what they notice out loud, and a coach answers. Fully on-device — no child's voice leaves the table.

**[SocratiDesk](https://github.com/Daidai1031/SocratiDesk)** · `Python` `LLM`
A voice-first study companion that asks instead of answers. No tabs, no copy-paste — just thinking aloud.

<br>

### 1:100 — In the room

**[ai-wardrobe](https://github.com/Daidai1031/ai-wardrobe)** · `Next.js` `Supabase` `Claude`
Photograph your closet once, then let it dress you for the weather.

<br>

### 1:5000 — Across the city

**[Into Place](https://github.com/Daidai1031/into-place)** · `TypeScript` `fal.ai`
Drop a pin, and a place's real archive becomes a short film you direct. Built on sourced historical material, not invented nostalgia — every generated frame stays labeled as generated.

<br>

### Unscaled — Systems underneath

**[teaguard-trust-api](https://github.com/Daidai1031/teaguard-trust-api)** · `Flask` `Groq`
A trust & safety backend for anonymous review platforms. It won't tell you whether a claim is true — it tells you which claims need a human to look.
